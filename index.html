About Me

I grew up in Northern Ireland County Down. From a young age I was always intrigued and curious in relation to ongoing technological advancements within the world, it all was very interesting for me, and that curiosity never left. I’m very much a physical person in relation to keeping myself fit and the enjoyment and passion I have for sports and self improvement, this is something I hope to integrate with my software development skill set within the future as I hope in the future to mix my passion and love for sport, with my passion for software development and the integration of automating new things within this vast and growing society we live in. My passion for technology stemmed from when I was a child and I always was intrigued with how systems worked such as old Nintendo systems (N64, Gamecube etc). The interest for older systems allowed myself to learn from the bottom up in terms of understanding old concepts then in tune with learning newer concepts within technology for nowadays.

With all of this, my first individual project was emulating an old gaming system OS onto a raspberry pie when I was in my early teens, this gave me a taste for starting and completing projects. From then most projects of my own (disregarding small/silly projects in my spare time) would be academic related and gave me insights to different IDE’s and different languages. I never joined the course Computer Science purely for the certification at the end of the tunnel, it was the possibilities of expanding my skill set to new heights and getting a taste of the industry is what roped me in and how glad I am for this decision. Overall, my passion for this field ran within me from a very young age and it shows through my demeanour and work ethic. In the future I wish to start new projects once I find myself available to start projects, such as a productivity app/piece of software, location based crime dashboard, a pandemic simulation, an AI chatbot (with Node.js) and many more so please stay posted to this website.

My Goals

My absolute main goal is to expand my skill set within this field/industry to it's maximum, and we all know this can only be done through new experiences and building new projects outside your comfort zone. This is why I love building things—whether it’s tools that make life easier, experiments with tech, or just weird little projects that spark curiosity. When I find the time, here’s some of the stuff I’d love to tackle:



Projects I’m Excited About:

• A Productivity App: Something simple but powerful—maybe for focus, habit-building, or just wrangling the chaos of daily tasks.

• Crime Data Dashboard: A clear, real-time map showing crime trends in different areas. I think data should be accessible, not hidden in spreadsheets.

• Pandemic Simulator: A sandbox to play with how diseases spread. Less "doom-scrolling news" and more "what if we tried this?"

• A Chatbot That Doesn’t Feel Robotic: Built with Node.js, because why not? Maybe for fun, maybe for practical use—we’ll see where it goes.



Other Random Ideas:

• A "Where’s My Money Going?" App: Budgeting tools for people who hate budgeting. Automatic insights, zero jargon.

• AR/VR Experiments: Silly or useful mini-games—like visualizing your Spotify playlist in 3D or a virtual tour of obscure historical spots.

• Neighbourhood Watch, But Digital: A place to share local updates, like lost pets, events, or which spots has the best wifi.

DIY Projects

There’s a special pride in building or fixing something yourself—even if it takes three trips to the hardware store and a YouTube tutorial playing at 0.5x speed. My projects range from "actually decent" to "well, it’s functional," but I love the process.



Animals & Wildlife

I’ve always been fascinated by monkeys—their intelligence, playfulness, and sheer chaos remind me of humans at our most unguarded. Whether it’s watching capuchins solve puzzles or howler monkeys belt out their (surprisingly loud) calls, I could observe them for hours.

My love for wildlife extends to the incredible biodiversity of South America—from the vibrant macaws of the Amazon to the elusive jaguars prowling the Pantanal. If there’s a documentary or a chance to see these animals in person, I’m there. One day, I’d love to trek through the rainforest or volunteer at a conservation project. Until then, I settle for obsessively following wildlife photographers and dreaming up future trips.
